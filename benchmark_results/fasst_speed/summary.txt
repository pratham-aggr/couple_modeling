  Caveats:

     unlike CAMulator's T62<->1deg transform.
   - FASST's atmosphere step includes the monthly ML sea-ice emulator and the
     ice-ocean flux weighting (inside 'post'); CAMulator's 220 ms does not.
   - The published CAMulator/GIAF/CESM2 numbers were measured by others on
     their own configurations; they are context, not a controlled comparison.
   - The 1-year 2CPU+1A100 segments include POP's own initialization (~39 s,
     1.6% of wall) inside the timed window, amortized over a single simulated
     year; this makes their SYPD slightly CONSERVATIVE.
   - The 2CPU+1A100 layout packs 128 POP ranks per node; the 4-GPU-node layout
     spreads the same 256 ranks over 4 nodes at 64/node, so it sees less
     per-node memory-bandwidth contention and runs ~5% faster per coupling
     step. Same core count and same single A100 in both cases.
